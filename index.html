QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Everything You Need to Know

Is the concert free to attend?

Yes! The concert is 100% free and outdoors right in front of the Regal Cinema at River Park in Fresno. Bring your family, grab a warm drink, and enjoy the holiday music!

Who can play in Big Brass Christmas?

Any low brass musician-tuba, sousaphone, euphonium, baritone, or trombone! We welcome from elementary school to college players, band directors, community musicians, and folks dusting off their horn for the holidays. Sign up with your email above and join our Facebook Group for rehearsal and call times.

What should performers bring on December 13th?

Bring your low brass instrument, chair, a folding music stand (plus clothespins or wind clips!), and festive holiday attire-Santa hats, ugly Christmas sweaters, and garland on your bell are part of the tradition!

How can I share this with my band director or friends?

Share bigbrasschristmas.com or download our printable 2026 Flyer PDF to post in your band room!
